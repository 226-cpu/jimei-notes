## 短语与搭配

- **pledge** — 作保证 / 承诺 / 誓言（可作名词或动词，具体用法见课文）
::: info 补充
/pledʒ/

n. 誓言，保证，承诺；捐款承诺；戒酒誓言（the pledge）；信物；质押；（美国大学中）宣誓加入男生（女生）联谊会的人；<古>为健康祝酒

As we set off, we must make this pledge that we shall alaways march ahead.

当我们出发，我们要做出一直向前的保证。

v. 保证，发誓；使保证，使许诺；承诺给予，承诺提供；用……抵押，以……典押；<美>宣誓加入（大学男生联谊会或女生联谊会）；<古>为健康祝酒

The new president pledged to cut taxes and increase employment.

新总统承诺要削减税收，增加就业。
:::

- **fascinating** — 迷人/令人着迷的
::: info 补充
/ˈfæsɪneɪtɪŋ/

adj. 极有吸引力的，迷人的

He is such a great writer that his stories are always fascinating.

他说是一个了不起的作家，他写的故事总是引人入胜。

v. 深深吸引，迷住（fascinate 的现在分词形式）。
:::

- **reap the benefits of** — 得享……的好处（`reap` 本义有「收获」）

::: info 补充
By learning to meditate, you can reap the benefits of this body state which include relaxation, energy and new prespectives on your life.

通过学习冥想，你的身体可以获得许多溢出，包括放松精力，甚至是对生活的新看法。
:::

- **overwhelm** — 压倒；使无法承受
::: info 补充
/əʊvəˈwelm/

<span class="术语">be overwhelmed by & be overwhelmed with</span>

If work or a problem overwhelmed someone, it is too much or too difficult to deal with.

如果工作或某个问题让某人感到不堪重负，那就意味着它太过繁重或过于困难，难以应对。

1. We were overwhelmed by a large amount of work.
工作多得让我们喘不过气来。

2. They would be overwhelmed with paperwork.
文案工作多得让他们应接不暇。

--------------------------------------------

If someone is overwhelmed by an emotion, they feel it so strongly that they cannot think clearly.

如果有人被某种情绪所淹没，那么他们就会强烈地感受到这种情绪，以至于无法清晰地思考了。

1. You might be overwhelmed with longing for those innocent days of early childhoos.

  你的内心充满了对你纯真童年时光得怀念。

2. You are so overwhelmed by a feeling of homesickness that you want to go home.

  你被一种想家的感觉压得喘不过气来，想要回家。

--------------------------------------------------
overwhelm also means to surprise someone very much so that they do not how to react.

“overwhelm”这个词还有另一层意思，即给某人带来极大的冲击，以至于他们不知该如何应对。

eg. I was completely overwhelmed by his generosity.

他的慷慨之举让我感到完全不知所措。
:::

- **emerge** — 出现；浮现；显现 (不及物动词)
::: info 补充
/ɪˈmɜːdʒ/

v.浮现，出现；显露，知悉；恢复过来，幸存下来；形成，兴起

第三人称单数 emerges

现在分词 emerging

过去式 emerged

过去分词 emerged

eg. 
1. No new leader has emerged totake his place.

    还没有新的领导来接替他。

2. The sun emerged from behind the clouds.

    太阳从云层后面露出脸来。

:::

- **stand a chance of** — 有机会 / 有希望（后接动名词或名词）
::: info 补充
eg.

  1. Students in part-time employment would also stand a chance of being exposed to different types of people, thus helping them to become mature when dealing with interpersonal relationships.

   从事兼职工作的学生也有机会接触到不同类型的人员，这有助于他们在处理人际关系时变得更加成熟。

  thus: 因此
:::


- **outweigh** — 在重要性、数量等方面超过（常接抽象名词）
::: info 补充
/aʊtˈweɪ/


  If one thing outweigh another. the first thing is of greater importance,benefit or significance than the second thing.

  如果某件事比另一件事更重要、更有益或更有意义，那么前者就比后者更为重要、更有利或更具意义。(重要性和意义上超过)

eg.

  At present, we are faced with unprecedented challenges and oppottunities, but the opportunities outweigh the challenges.

  当前我们面临前所未有的机遇和挑战，但机遇大于挑战。

:::


## 语法要点

::: info 补充
In welcoming you to the university, I am reminded of my own high school graducation and the photograph my mom took of my dad and me.

在欢迎各位来到这所大学之际，我回想起了自己高中毕业的情景，以及母亲为我和父亲拍摄的那张照片。

- “In + ving” indicates that when you do something, something else happens as a result.

    在…过程中

    eg.

    1. In trying to realize your personal dreams, have you ever thought of doing something for other people?

        在实现你个人梦想的过程中，你可曾想过为他人做点什么？

    In trying to realize your personal dreams => When you try to realize your personal dreams

---------------------------------------
    
- remind sb of sb./sth.

    make someone remember sb. that they konw or sth. that happened in the pase.

    eg.

    1. The song reminds me of our holiday in Brisbane.

    那首歌总让我想起我们在布里斯班的假期。 


:::
